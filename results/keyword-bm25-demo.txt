LIMITATION DEMO — same concept, different wording:
  Query 'what is a firm worth' (paraphrase of "enterprise value")
  -> ['no matches']
  The valuation material answers this question (enterprise value is estimated
  from free cash flows), yet BM25 finds nothing: the words do not overlap.

Note: a first attempt with 'how much is a company worth' unexpectedly matched
the marketing doc — because 'how' is not in bm25s's default 'en' stopword
list and both texts contain it. Stopword coverage is a lever worth tuning.

Conclusion: BM25 is exact-term retrieval. Semantically related content
expressed with different vocabulary (paraphrases, synonyms, abbreviations)
is missed, which is why Phase 2 also builds a text-embedding index over
the same chunks — embeddings retrieve by meaning, not by word overlap.
